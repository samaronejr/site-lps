# Editorial workflow

## Prepare, review, and publish

1. A contributor creates a draft with the source record, provenance, collection owner, last review
   date, and known uncertainty. A contributor cannot publish or self-approve.
2. The assigned section editor checks the source-priority policy, factual scope, review/expiry
   state, relationships, accessibility requirements, and archive status. Unsupported or unresolved
   claims remain draft.
3. For localized material, a translator edits only localized editorial fields. Shared identifiers,
   canonical dates, relations, and source records are not translation fields. A section editor who
   did not produce that translation reviews it before it can be published. Required bilingual
   material has no mixed-language fallback; Portuguese is authoritative and English is a reviewed
   variant.
4. The section editor sends a complete reviewed revision to a publisher. A distinct publisher with
   MFA publishes, schedules, unpublishes, or restores it. The action is attributable and reversible.
5. The collection owner reviews published material at the matrix cadence and sooner when its source,
   status, deadline, public contact, privacy state, rights, or accessibility state changes.

No role may use a shared account. The deployer deploys approved artifacts only and has no editorial
content-editing path. The administrator manages individual accounts and settings without replacing
editorial or privacy review.

## Correction and takedown

A correction request is logged with the claimed issue, affected record/asset, source supplied,
received time, and disposition. The section editor triages factual corrections against the
source-priority policy. A publisher releases a reviewed correction and preserves a revision trail.

A takedown, privacy, rights, or public-contact request goes to the publisher and privacy auditor.
They can restrict or unpublish the public item while evidence is reviewed; an administrator handles
only the necessary technical access or configuration action. Do not silently alter provenance or
hard-delete a referenced record. Preserve a restricted internal record of the request and action
where retention is permitted and confirmed by the applicable institutional policy.

No public email or form route is assumed. Before launch, LPS must establish and publish an owned
correction/takedown route. Until then, the missing route is a launch blocker even though the
internal role route is defined.

## Freshness, expiry, and archive

The matrix's ISO review cadence is the maximum ordinary interval, not a grace period. Any triggering
change requires earlier review. Opportunities and events derive their public state from dates;
closed opportunities retain a stable page and become noindex after 90 days. Events retain explicit
cancelled or postponed status. News is presented as dated material, not silently refreshed as
current information.

Archived records retain stable historical relationships, source/provenance, dates, and a clear
archived or historical state. Referenced records are archived rather than hard-deleted. A redirect
is retained as a redirect or deliberate 410 decision and must not create a chain. Archive status
never makes outdated facts eligible for active listings.

## Legacy documents and PDFs

A legacy PDF is discovery evidence, not a publication authorization. Essential public information
must have an accessible HTML summary and context; it cannot be available only in an inaccessible
PDF. An essential inaccessible PDF remains draft, is remediated, or is replaced by a sourced
accessible HTML equivalent before public release. A nonessential historical PDF may be represented
as restricted or archived metadata when rights and access review require it, but it must not be used
to support current claims or to evade accessibility obligations.

## Evidence that cannot be guessed

Do not infer a person's public email, photo rights, consent, legal basis, source authority,
translation approval, publisher approval, or collection owner from a legacy page, filename, role,
or URL. Record documentary evidence and its provenance. If evidence is absent, keep the record or
field non-public and report the launch blocker.
