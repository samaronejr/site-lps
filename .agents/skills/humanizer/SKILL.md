# Humanizer

Goal: copy that reads like a person at the laboratory wrote it — factual,
direct, slightly boring. If a sentence could appear on any AI-generated
landing page on the internet, it fails.

## Strip these AI tells

1. **Meta-commentary about the artifact itself.** The text talks about the
   site, the redesign, the migration, or the writing ("não é uma decisão de
   design", "este redesenho", "trabalho de migração", "bloqueios de
   lançamento registrados no material de migração"). Readers never see the
   process — delete it or convert it into a plain fact.
2. **Justifications addressed to the reader.** "exige responsável
   institucional", "o laboratório não publica uma lista que não pode
   verificar", "isso garante transparência". Don't explain your standards;
   state what exists or what the visitor can do.
3. **Inflated significance.** "desempenha papel fundamental", "marca um
   compromisso", "testament to", "underscores the importance". If the claim
   can't be falsified, it's decoration — cut it.
4. **The rule of three.** Three parallel adjectives/nouns stacked for rhythm
   ("pesquisa, ensino e extensão" is fine when it's literally the scope;
   "inovador, dinâmico e colaborativo" is filler).
5. **"Não apenas X — Y" / "not just X — Y" pivots** and em-dash aphorisms.
6. **Hedged vague authority.** "é reconhecido", "amplamente considerado",
   "diversas iniciativas", "várias parcerias" with no name, number, or date.
   Either name the thing or delete the claim.
7. **Throat-clearing openings.** "Em um mundo cada vez mais", "No cenário
   atual", "É importante notar que". Start on the subject.
8. **Question-then-answer scaffolding.** "O que o laboratório faz? O
   laboratório desenvolve..." — just say it.
9. **Buzzword stacking and nominalization chains.** "ecossistema de
   inovação", "sinergia entre competências", "viabilizar o fortalecimento".
   Prefer the concrete verb: "financia", "oferece", "mantém".
10. **Uniform rhythm.** AI text is metronomic: same-length sentences,
    symmetrical paragraphs. Vary length; let a short sentence land.

## Write like this

- **Concrete subject + concrete verb.** "O laboratório mantém três linhas
  de pesquisa" beats "Nosso trabalho abrange um amplo espectro".
- **Facts over praise.** Numbers, names, dates. "Fundado em 1985" not
  "longa trajetória". If you don't have the fact, don't gesture at it.
- **Honest emptiness.** An empty state says what is missing and where to
  ask: "Ainda não há publicações listadas nesta página. Para informações,
  contate a secretaria." Never fill the void with philosophy about why the
  void is principled.
- **Say what the visitor can do.** Read, apply, contact, download.
- **Institutional voice, not marketing.** Third person or "o laboratório";
  no "nosso time incrível", no exclamation marks, no calls to excitement.
- **Short over clever.** If two phrasings survive, keep the shorter.

## Bilingual rule (this site)

PT is authoritative; EN mirrors it faithfully — no embellishment that only
exists in one locale, same facts in both, similar length. Idiomatic EN
(say "the laboratory", "the lab") but never friendlier than the PT.

## Checklist before shipping a string

- [ ] Could this sentence appear unchanged on a thousand other sites? → rewrite
- [ ] Does it mention the site, the redesign, migration, or "the list"? → rewrite
- [ ] Does it assert a quality with no fact attached? → attach a fact or delete
- [ ] Does it explain to the reader how principled the lab is? → delete
- [ ] Is the EN a mirror of the PT, not an improvisation? → fix
