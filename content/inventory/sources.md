# LPS public-source inventory

Captured on **2026-08-30 at 23:03:43 UTC**. This is a migration triage inventory, not an editorial approval or a claim that archived material remains true.

## Method and field contract

Only anonymous public HTTP, DNS, GitHub API, and Internet Archive CDX/availability endpoints were used. No Google Drive document was opened, no authenticated surface was accessed, and no private resource was requested after an authentication wall was observed.

All CSV rows carry a source URL, UTC capture timestamp, provenance, candidate owner role, locale, freshness, rights/privacy state, SHA-256 checksum, and one of `migrate`, `link`, `301`, `410`, or `private-excluded`. Candidate owners are roles, not invented assignments. `und` means the language was not established from the archive index; `zxx` marks non-linguistic assets.

Checksums mean:

- current page/record rows: SHA-256 of the captured response or deterministic source-evidence tuple;
- archive URL rows: SHA-256 of the CDX timestamp, original URL, status, and archive digest tuple;
- assets: SHA-256 of transiently fetched public response bytes. The bytes were deleted immediately and are not stored here.

URL normalization lowercases scheme/host, removes default ports/fragments and terminal non-root slashes, and sorts query parameters. `urls.csv` is de-duplicated on that value. Asset checksum collisions are reported by the checker for duplicate review; none are silently discarded.

## Disposition policy

- `migrate`: current institutional records/pages that warrant review and structured migration.
- `link`: externally maintained public profiles, repositories, documentation, or rights-uncleared assets.
- `301`: legacy URL with a plausible successor content class; final targets remain a later IA/migration decision.
- `410`: obsolete utility, library, event, or miscellaneous archive URL without a justified current successor.
- `private-excluded`: administration paths or Google Sites that redirected to sign-in. Nothing behind those walls was accessed.

Every URL discovered from the bounded source set above appears once in `urls.csv` with a disposition. `coverage.json`, generated by `npm run qa:inventory`, is the machine report and must show zero unclassified URLs.

## Rights and privacy

Public email addresses observed in source material were not inventoried as assets or copied into records.
