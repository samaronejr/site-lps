CPE-886 · Graduate

Quantum Machine Learning

Graduate course at PEE/COPPE on the fundamentals of quantum computing and quantum machine-learning algorithms.

Course

About the course

Professor
João Victor da Fonseca Pinto
Program
PEE/COPPE
Offering
First semester of 2026 · Concluded
Period
March 23, 2026 to July 4, 2026
Venue
COPPE/UFRJ — Bloco H, sala 220

In two parts — quantum computing fundamentals and quantum machine-learning algorithms — the course uses Qiskit and PennyLane and combines slides, notebooks and assignments with seminars and an extra-point challenge.

Syllabus

Course contents

  • Part I — quantum computing fundamentals
  • Part II — quantum machine-learning algorithms
  • Tooling: Qiskit and PennyLane
  • Assignments, seminars and the extra-point challenge

References

Bibliography

  • Machine Learning with Quantum Computers, Schuld and Petruccione, Springer, 2021

  • Machine Learning: Classical and Quantum, E. Combarro, 2023
