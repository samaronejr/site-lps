EEL710 · Undergraduate

Instrumentation and Measurement Techniques

Undergraduate course at Poli/UFRJ covering theoretical and practical aspects of instrumentation and measurement in electrical engineering.

Course

About the course

Professor
João Victor da Fonseca Pinto
Program
Electronic and Computer Engineering (Poli/UFRJ)
Offering
Second semester of 2026 · In progress
Period
August 3, 2026 to December 19, 2026
Venue
COPPE/UFRJ — Bloco H, sala 220

The course presents measurement theory applied to electrical engineering measurements, covering statistical parameters, sensors, electronic measurement instruments and the criteria and tools used in instrumentation project design. Offered at Poli/UFRJ, the course is organized into thematic units with supporting material published on the course page.

Syllabus

Course contents

  • Definition and purpose of measurement
  • International system of units
  • Statistical characterization of measurements
  • Error propagation
  • Calibration protocols
  • Distribution analysis
  • Regression analysis
  • Hypothesis testing
  • Sensors and transducers
  • Measurement instruments
  • Instrumentation design
