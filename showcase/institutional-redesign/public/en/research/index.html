Research

Research areas

The main areas of activity are electronic instrumentation, analog and digital signal processing, software engineering and computational intelligence.

Areas

Six research fronts

Digital signal processing

Filtering, compression, estimation and detection of signals in noisy, high-rate environments.

  • Adaptive filtering
  • Signal compression
  • Energy estimation
  • Novelty detection

Computational intelligence

Shallow and deep neural networks, kernel learning and generative AI applied to engineering problems.

  • Deep learning
  • Kernel learning
  • Ensemble learning
  • Autoencoders

Data modelling and analysis

Supervised and unsupervised modelling, feature engineering, time series and recommender systems.

  • Feature engineering
  • Time series
  • Recommender systems
  • Data quality

Detection, failure and monitoring

Failure, fraud and novelty detection, digital twins and monitoring of critical systems.

  • Anomaly detection
  • Digital twin
  • Financial time series
  • Process safety

Experimental high-energy physics

UFRJ–CERN collaboration since 1988: event simulation, reconstruction and online filtering in the ATLAS experiment.

  • Event simulation
  • Online triggering
  • Calorimetry
  • Particle identification

Sonar and acoustic signals

Underwater acoustic signal processing, contact classification and synthetic acoustic data.

  • Passive sonar
  • Towed array sonar
  • Signal classification
  • Acoustic simulators

Projects

Selected projects

All projects

Online electron filtering at ATLAS

Online filtering system for the ATLAS experiment (CERN), with neural-network electron classification — the first time a neural method took part in the online electron selection of the experiment.

  • Trigger
  • Neural networks
  • HEP
2018 —

Event simulation in HEP

Simulation and reconstruction of high-energy physics events, with workload orchestration for large data volumes.

  • Simulation
  • Workflows
  • Scientific computing
2018 —

Passive sonar and underwater acoustic signals

Novelty detection, contact classification, target tracking and towed-array sonar technology, in partnership with the Brazilian Navy Research Institute.
