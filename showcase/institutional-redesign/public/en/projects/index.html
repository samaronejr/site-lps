Research

Projects

Research and development projects recorded in the laboratory public material, with the partner institutions they were built with.

Source: the laboratory public pages, January 2025.

What this list is, and what it is not

The public project index names two initiatives (HEP event simulation and the ATLAS online filtering system) and the faculty pages describe the applied projects below. A complete, current portfolio requires laboratory review.

Online electron filtering at ATLAS

Online filtering system for the ATLAS experiment (CERN), with neural-network electron classification — the first time a neural method took part in the online electron selection of the experiment.

  • Trigger
  • Neural networks
  • HEP
2018 — · CERN · ATLAS, UFRJPublic source

Event simulation in HEP

Simulation and reconstruction of high-energy physics events, with workload orchestration for large data volumes.

  • Simulation
  • Workflows
  • Scientific computing
2018 — · CERN · ATLASPublic source

Passive sonar and underwater acoustic signals

Novelty detection, contact classification, target tracking and towed-array sonar technology, in partnership with the Brazilian Navy Research Institute.

  • Sonar
  • Defence
  • Deep learning
2016 — · Brazilian Navy, Navy Research InstitutePublic source

Passive seismic monitoring of the Mero field

Reservoir monitoring with advanced signal analysis techniques, within the Libra consortium.

  • Oil and gas
  • Seismic
  • Time series
2020 — · Libra Consortium, PetrobrasPublic source

Industrial digital twin for process safety

Digital-twin prototype focused on process safety in an offshore plant, integrating data, computational models and machine learning.

  • Digital twin
  • MLOps
  • Operational risk
2021 — · CENPES/PetrobrasPublic source

Barrier degradation and probabilistic accident modelling

Bringing artificial intelligence into dynamic process-safety barrier management, supporting decision-making in critical scenarios.

  • Reliability
  • Probabilistic modelling
  • AI
2019 — · CENPES/PetrobrasPublic source
