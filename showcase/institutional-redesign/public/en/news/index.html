    Coordination of the ATLAS electron and photon online filtering group

    LPS coordinated the international research group on online filtering for electrons and photons in the ATLAS experiment.

    First neural network in ATLAS online electron selection

    A collaborative effort upgraded the online electron classification based on artificial neural networks — a contribution of major importance to the experiment.
