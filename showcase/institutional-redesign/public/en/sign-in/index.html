Sign in

The restricted area is for professors, laboratory staff and site administrators. Access is individual, requires a second factor for privileged roles and is recorded for audit.

I forgot my password

Design preview: this form is a demonstration and authenticates nobody. In the published version it posts the credentials to WordPress on the same domain, and the response is the professor's working area.

Who signs in here

  • Professors and researchers

    Maintain their own page: the courses they teach, classes, lessons and notes. What is published appears on the professor's public page.

  • Laboratory staff

    Maintain news, projects, publications and the material archive.

  • Site administrators

    Review content, manage roles and take care of the site configuration.

First access and help

  1. The account is created by the laboratory office, with the institutional email address.
  2. On first access, the second factor is enrolled in an authenticator app; without it the professor or administrator role is not granted.
  3. Password recovery uses the link below, on the site's own domain.
  4. Access problems are handled by secretaria@lps.ufrj.br; never share your password.
